# Nagrik Party Online System Brief

Source: party registration, constitution, rulebook, manifesto, governance, ethics, digital governance, grievance, finance, candidate, internal democracy, media, legal aid, women safety, youth, education, environment, and policy research papers supplied on 2026-06-06.

## Current Status

Nagrik Party is not registered yet. The first product phase must therefore help the founding team prepare, verify, organise, and track the registration process. Until official registration is granted, public copy and system labels should describe the organisation as a proposed party / party under registration, not as a registered political party.

After registration, the same platform should graduate into the party operating system for ground work, internal governance, member coordination, public accountability, and transparent civic operations.

Important electoral milestone distinction:

- Section 29A registration is the first legal registration milestone.
- Recognised State party status is a later electoral-performance milestone under the Election Symbols framework.
- The product should say "registered political party" only after Section 29A registration, and "recognised State party" only after ECI recognition criteria are actually met.

## Geographic Strategy

Nagrik Party should be Delhi-focused first.

Launch geography:

- Delhi, across all 70 Assembly constituencies.

Anchor constituency:

- Okhla Assembly Constituency, Delhi.
- Okhla is the Party President's 2025 independent contest constituency and should be treated as the first public proof point in the President's journey, not as a limitation on the party's launch scope.

Expansion model:

- Launch as a Delhi-based party formation from day one.
- Build Delhi-wide issue intake, manifesto priorities, volunteer signup, and membership application flows.
- Give every Delhi Vidhan Sabha a public area page, even if early data is sparse.
- Use Okhla as the first richer constituency story because the President has already contested there.
- Expand operating depth constituency by constituency as volunteers, independent candidates, and local teams come together.
- Treat the platform as a system where independent candidates and civic local leaders can organise under a common constitutional party structure.
- Broader state/national expansion should wait until the Delhi model has proven field operations and after formal registration/recognition milestones justify expansion.

## Product Interpretation

Nagrik Party is not primarily describing a marketing website. The documents define a citizen-first Delhi political institution that needs a formation and operations system built around visible governance: registration readiness, document control, founding member records, issue intake, ward-level response, escalation, transparency dashboards, internal democracy, verified membership, ethical communication, public finance visibility, candidate scrutiny, and representative accountability.

The useful v1 should therefore be a formation-to-operations platform with a public layer, not a brochure site.

## Independent Candidate Platform Model

Nagrik Party should be presented as a constitutional home for independent candidates, civic leaders, local organisers, professionals, and public-service volunteers who want disciplined party infrastructure without opaque high-command politics.

Candidate pipeline:

- Independent or local civic leader expresses interest.
- Local team and issue history are mapped.
- Candidate accepts constitution, ethics, financial transparency, and public accountability standards.
- Candidate receives a public profile and constituency work dashboard.
- Candidate's constituency manifesto is built from verified local issues, public priorities, and member/volunteer voting.

This keeps the movement credible: the party is not merely recruiting candidates, it is standardising public accountability before elections.

Delhi candidate strategy:

- The public product should make room for independent candidates across Delhi to express interest.
- Candidate onboarding should be invitation/review-based, not an open automatic ticket claim.
- Every candidate profile should connect to that constituency's issues, public work, local manifesto priorities, and transparency commitments.
- The party should avoid presenting candidates as official party nominees before registration and formal selection procedures are complete.

## Funding Transition

The bank account used for the Delhi 2025 independent election and submitted to ECI should be tracked in the system as an existing compliance/election account record.

Important compliance note:

- ECI instructions for candidates describe a separate bank account opened exclusively for election expenditure during a candidate's election.
- Before using that account for party public funding, a CA/election lawyer should confirm whether it can legally be repurposed, whether the bank account name/status supports party funding, and whether a separate party/association bank account is required before or after registration.

Product handling:

- Until confirmed, the site should not expose public donation collection through that account.
- The app can collect funding interest/pledges or supporter intent separately.
- Once legally cleared, the app can enable a public funding module with receipts, donor records, public summaries, and compliance exports.
- Public funding copy must avoid implying 80GGC/tax benefits or registered-party donation status before registration is complete and legally confirmed.

## Manifesto Product Model

The manifesto is not just a static PDF. It should have two layers:

1. National manifesto
   A structured presentation of what the party stands for: visible governance, public infrastructure, women safety, healthcare, legal accessibility, youth/employment, environment, digital governance, transparency, and local democracy.

2. Area manifesto
   Each Vidhan Sabha should eventually get its own living manifesto generated from verified local issues, public priorities, and volunteer/member voting.

Pre-registration voting:

- Volunteers and members can upvote manifesto proposals.
- The site can show a public Top 10 list.
- Voting should be auditable and limited to signed-in supporters/members.
- Public display should show counts and ranking, not voter identities.

## Issues Product Model

The Issues page is the core pre-election accountability engine.

Public issue submission should allow:

- photo upload
- issue type selection
- description
- location/geotag
- automatic Vidhan Sabha assignment
- automatic ward assignment where boundary data is available
- optional contact details
- public anonymous display

Public issue display should:

- never show complainant name, email, phone, or precise personal identity
- show issue photo, category, public location label, ward/Vidhan Sabha, status, and timestamps
- support status such as submitted, verified, complaint filed, escalated, unresolved, added to area manifesto, resolved

Political workflow:

- Citizens submit local problems.
- Volunteers verify and classify issues.
- Before elections, Nagrik Party files official complaints through lawful channels.
- If unresolved, the issue can be added to the relevant Vidhan Sabha manifesto.
- After electoral victory, these issues become the local delivery backlog.

## Volunteer And Membership Distinction

Volunteer signup and legal party membership must be separate.

Volunteer / supporter:

- Can help with issue verification, outreach, translation, local research, event support, and digital tasks.
- Should not be represented as a legally enrolled party member unless they complete the membership process.
- I did not find an ECI-prescribed volunteer form for political party registration in the official registration guidelines. The volunteer form should therefore be party-designed and consent-based.

Legal party member:

- Must be an adult Indian citizen.
- Should affirm acceptance of the Party Constitution and Rulebook.
- Should affirm they are not a member of another political party registered with ECI.
- Should provide elector details needed for registration evidence where applicable.
- For the first registration-ready set, the app should track the ECI-required 100+ members with electoral proof/EPIC and affidavit status.

ECI-aligned membership fields to track:

- full name
- parent/spouse name where needed for affidavit/legal matching
- age/date of birth
- residential address
- email
- phone, even if login is email-only, because offline legal coordination may need it
- Indian citizenship confirmation
- age 18+ confirmation
- registered elector confirmation
- EPIC number or electoral roll reference
- Assembly Constituency name/number
- current political party membership declaration
- acceptance of Party Constitution and Rulebook
- consent for legal verification and party membership processing
- affidavit required / received / notarised status
- electoral proof required / received / attested status

Office bearer records need stricter tracking for PAN, income tax return / income details, asset-liability affidavit status, criminal antecedent affidavit status, and role.

## Core Operating Doctrine

- "Kaam dikhna chahiye" means every workflow should produce a visible record: issue reported, volunteer assigned, escalation logged, representative response captured, public status updated, report published.
- "People's Opposition" means the system should help the party collect public problems, verify them, escalate them lawfully, and publish accountability without exposing sensitive citizen data.
- "Hybrid Democratic Governance" means digital participation supports physical units and delegate systems. The app should not assume all democratic decisions happen online.
- "Transparency with safeguards" means public dashboards are important, but complainant identity, women safety cases, legal aid requests, whistleblower reports, and internal disciplinary processes need strict privacy controls.

## Two-Phase Product Strategy

### Phase 1: Registration Command Centre

This phase helps the founding team get the party registered and build institutional credibility before official registration.

1. Registration checklist
   Track ECI/Section 29A preparation items, required documents, status, responsible person, due date, review status, and submission readiness.

2. Document vault
   Store constitution, rulebook, affidavits, resolutions, member lists, office bearer lists, PAN/bank documents, publication proofs, legal drafts, and version history.

3. Founding member and office bearer registry
   Maintain verified founder/member records, identity status, address/contact details, role, unit, signed declarations, and eligibility notes.

4. Compliance evidence tracker
   Track documents that prove internal democracy, constitutional commitment, non-discrimination, peaceful functioning, financial compliance, and authorised signatories.

5. Registration timeline
   Track formation date, internal resolutions, document finalisation, public notices, submission milestones, objections/queries, ECI correspondence, and final registration decision.

6. Public pre-registration page
   Present the proposed party's values, documents approved for public release, founder statements, and citizen interest/volunteer signups without claiming registered status.

7. Public issue and manifesto pipeline
   Collect public problems, allow signed-in supporter/member upvotes, create Top 10 priorities, and prepare area-wise manifesto inputs.

### Phase 2: Nagrik Ground OS

After registration, the platform becomes the operating system for the party.

1. Public civic issue reporting
   Citizens can submit issues by category, location, description, photos, urgency, and optional contact details.

2. Ward and constituency response console
   Ward volunteers and coordinators can verify, assign, comment, escalate, update status, and record evidence.

3. Public accountability dashboard
   Public visitors can see aggregated issue counts, open/resolved status, category trends, ward-level heat, representative work summaries, and published public reports without personal data.

4. Member and volunteer system
   Users can register as members or volunteers, join local units, receive roles, and participate in tasks.

5. Internal audit trail
   Every sensitive action gets an immutable event trail: status change, assignment, escalation, public visibility change, finance entry, candidate review update.

6. Official communication layer
   Admins can publish authorised announcements, corrections, public reports, and campaign updates with role-based approval.
